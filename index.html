Got questions?

We've got answers ✦

When is the cut-off for same-day delivery? +
Orders placed before 12pm (noon) qualify for same-day delivery, arriving by 5pm. After 12pm, we'll deliver the following morning between 9am–1pm. You'll receive a tracking update when your bouquet is on its way.
How long will the flowers last? +
Most bouquets last 5–10 days with proper care. Every order includes a care card — trim stems at a 45° angle, use fresh cool water, change it every 2 days, and keep away from direct sunlight.
Can I add a personalised message? +
Yes! Select the "Card message +$8" add-on when customising your bouquet. You'll enter your message at checkout and our florist will handwrite it on a botanical card included with the arrangement.
What if my flowers arrive damaged? +
We stand behind every bouquet with our Fresh Flower Guarantee. If anything arrives less than perfect, send us a photo within 24 hours and we'll replace the bouquet or issue a full refund — no questions asked.
Do you offer subscription or repeat orders? +
Not yet — but it's coming soon! Subscribe to our newsletter to be first to know. In the meantime, email us and we can arrange a recurring order manually.
Can I customise the flowers in a bouquet? +
Each bouquet has a signature design, but you can fully customise the size, wrap, ribbon, and add-ons on every product page. For fully bespoke arrangements with specific flower requests, contact us via Instagram or email.
