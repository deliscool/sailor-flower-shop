Sailor Flower Power Shop was born from a single belief: that flowers are more than decoration — they are a language. One stem can say I love you, another I see you, another simply here, take this moment of beauty. We are here to help you speak that language fluently.

Philosophy

Seasonally Sourced

We work only with what the season gives us — no forced blooms, no synthetic colours. Every bouquet reflects the time of year it was made, which means it's always fresh and always honest.

Each arrangement is assembled by hand, stem by stem. We don't rush, we don't template. Your bouquet is made the same morning it's delivered — never a day before.

The Beginning

From a market stall to something magical

It started small — a farmers' market stall, a folding table, twelve bunches of dahlias wrapped in brown paper. The founder, Yari, had just left a career in design and wanted to make things with her hands again. She had no grand plan. She just loved flowers, and she thought other people might too.

Twelve years later, Sailor Flower Power Shop is still small on purpose. We don't want to be everywhere. We want to be exactly here — a place where every order is received as a joy, and every bouquet leaves as an act of care.

The name? Yari's daughter gave it to her, age 6, after a morning spent watching her work. It stuck.
